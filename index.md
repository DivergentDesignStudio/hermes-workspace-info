# Hermes Workspace — personal integration

This is a personal Google Workspace integration operated by the owner of the DivergentDesignStudio GitHub account using Hermes Agent. It is not a Google product and is not offered as a public service.

## What it does

With the account owner's explicit Google authorization, Hermes can help read, send and organize Gmail; manage calendar events; read and manage Drive files; read contacts; and read or edit Google Sheets and Docs. Actions are performed in response to the owner's requests. The requested Google OAuth permissions support these features.

## Privacy and control

Google account data and OAuth credentials are not published on this website or in its repository. The integration runs on the owner's computer. Requested content may be processed by the AI model provider configured in Hermes, as explained in the [privacy policy](privacy.html).

Access can be revoked at any time in [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
